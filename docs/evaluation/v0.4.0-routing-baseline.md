# v0.4.0 routing baseline

This public-safe baseline records a routing observation before the two dedicated
v0.4.0 skills were added. No private model files, local paths, engineering
values, or agent-identifying details are included.

The staged-construction task could be composed from the dependency-preflight
and ordinary analysis-step workflows. The mapped-load task could be composed
from the ordinary-load and controlled-export workflows. That composition was
useful but left the primary route and expected output shape implicit.

The v0.4.0 skills therefore standardize one narrow primary route and one static
finding contract:

- `abaqus-staged-construction-auditor` owns `construction_events` validation and
  `C-STAGE-001`; ordinary procedure and increment questions remain with
  `abaqus-step`.
- `abaqus-mapped-load-provenance-auditor` owns `mapped_loads` provenance and
  `C-MAPLOAD-001`; ordinary magnitude/direction questions remain with
  `abaqus-load`, and approved artifact movement remains with `abaqus-export`.

Both routes are read-only static audits. A passing finding does not establish
solver completion, physical correctness, or engineering approval.

## Independent blind routing check

Two fresh, read-only routing rounds were run after the v0.4.0 skill set was
assembled. The evaluators received only synthetic prompts and the public skill
instructions; they were not given the expected routes or this evaluation note.

Across both rounds, dedicated `construction_events` prompts selected
`abaqus-staged-construction-auditor`, mapped-source provenance and face-count
prompts selected `abaqus-mapped-load-provenance-auditor`, ordinary pressure
review stayed with `abaqus-load`, and response-variable planning stayed with
`abaqus-output`. Ordinary procedure and increment review stayed with
`abaqus-step`. Every response preserved the read-only boundary and explicitly
withheld solver, physical-correctness, and engineering-approval claims.
