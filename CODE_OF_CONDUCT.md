# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and respect.
- Give and accept constructive technical feedback.
- Protect private engineering information and report safety concerns responsibly.
- Focus discussion on reproducible evidence and the public project scope.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats,
doxing, publishing private information, or sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and may temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate. Report
conduct concerns privately through the repository's security-reporting channel.
Maintainers will protect reporter privacy as far as practical and will apply
consequences proportionately and consistently.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
